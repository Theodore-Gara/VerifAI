# Reward artwork credits

Every picture in this folder is **original artwork made for VerifAI** with an AI image
generator (Google Gemini and Lucid Origin), prompted by the team for this project.
Nothing here is stock photography, so there is no third-party licence to clear and no
attribution obligation for distribution with the app.

The pictures were delivered as 1024–3000 px squares and are stored here at 900 × 900 px,
progressive JPEG, quality 84 (33–105 KB each) so the whole catalogue adds under 700 KB to
the app bundle.

## Notes for reviewers

- The products are **concepts, not live offers**. No partner, code or donation is attached
  to any redemption; the reward `note` field says so on each product page.
- Brand-looking objects (the controller, the `$50` card, the productivity software name)
  are illustrative only. VerifAI is not affiliated with, endorsed by or sponsored by any
  of those brands, and no logo is used as an app asset.
- Keep the photos as JPEGs. Catalogue values in `rewards.image_asset` reference the file
  name only (`reward_coffee.jpg`); `RewardImage` prepends `assets/images/`.
