VerifAI welcome voice asset

The splash screen is wired to look for this file on first launch:
  assets/audio/verifai_welcome_voice.mp3

Record the approved welcome in Mpho's own voice, save/export it as MP3 with
that exact filename, and place it in this folder. The app will attempt to play
it automatically once on first launch. Until the recording is supplied, the
app falls back to the included verifai_welcome_chime.wav. No synthetic or
imitation voice is included.

Suggested script:
"Hi there! Welcome to VerifAI. I'm so glad you're here. I'm your AI learning
assistant, here to help you learn, explore, and verify information. Remember,
AI assists, humans verify. Let's get started!"
